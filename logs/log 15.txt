Maintenance policy
- Keep active listing snapshots for 48 hours.
- Keep raw completed sales for 30 days.
- Keep candles and current market_stats.
- Run WAL checkpointing after cleanup.
- Run VACUUM weekly, not every day.

Reasoning
- Listing snapshots are the highest-growth low-value table.
- Completed sales are more valuable, so they have a longer retention window.
- Candles are compact and useful for long-term charting.
- VACUUM rewrites the SQLite database, so it should be infrequent.
