Filled map removal - 2026-08-17

Reason:
Filled maps are player-created artwork. Donut's API generalizes them as
minecraft:filled_map, so combining all filled maps into one market creates
misleading and not-useful price data.

Change:
Added minecraft:filled_map to the dashboard excluded item list.

Effect:
Filled maps are now filtered from:
- search
- market tables
- movers/gainers/losers
- opportunities
- direct item detail responses
- account transaction summaries and account sales lists

The raw collector data is not deleted. The dashboard simply stops showing filled
maps as a market item.

Verification:
- python3 -m py_compile dashboard/server.py passed.
- dashboard service restarted and is active.
- Direct function checks showed:
  search_filled_map_hits=0
  market_filled_map_hits=0
  opportunity_filled_map_hits=0
  direct_item_detail_is_none=True
