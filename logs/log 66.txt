Question answered:
- Usual Sell Price is based on completed auction sales, meaning prices players actually paid recently.
- Average Listing is based on current items listed for sale, meaning prices sellers are asking for right now.
- These can differ because listed items may be overpriced, stale, underpriced, or not sold yet.
