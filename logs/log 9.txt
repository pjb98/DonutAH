Important finding
- Data collection is active and current, but the service is not achieving the intended 5-second cycle time.
- Recent journal entries show cycles completing roughly every 2-3 minutes, not every 5 seconds.
- Each cycle still fetches 1,000 transactions and 352 listing rows.
- Recent cycles inserted roughly 875-979 new sales each time, which suggests the collector may be missing sales during high-volume periods.

Likely cause
- The collector recalculates market_stats and refreshes recent candles inside each hot ingestion cycle.
- As the database grows, those aggregation jobs are slowing the transaction polling loop.

Recommended next fix
- Split ingestion from aggregation:
  - Hot path every 5 seconds: fetch transaction pages 1-10, upsert sales, do minimal gap metrics.
  - Rolling listings: scan pages on a separate budget.
  - Aggregation worker every 1-5 minutes: refresh candles and market_stats outside the transaction poll loop.
